Subject
From the classroom to the sales floor: why I work with data

Hello,

I started out teaching mathematics. At ODA Class I taught 35,000+ students across Grades 1–10 in live online classes, with about 95% positive feedback. Teaching at that scale showed me the patterns behind the numbers: attendance, engagement, quiz scores and where students dropped off. I started analysing them, and that pulled me into data.

Today I'm a Data Analyst in PMO & Strategy at Unacademy (PrepLadder). I build what a sales organisation runs on: hourly reports for leadership, run-rate forecasts, incentive models, and the data checks that keep every number honest.

One thing hasn't changed. A good analysis, like a good lesson, ends with someone knowing exactly what to do next.
